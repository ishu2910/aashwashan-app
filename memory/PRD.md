### Adding a Blog Post
1. Login to Admin Panel
2. Click on "Manage Blogs"
3. Click "Create New Post"
4. Fill in:
   - Title
   - Content (supports markdown)
   - Category
   - Featured Image URL
5. Click "Publish"
6. Share the blog link: `https://aashwashan-app.preview.emergentagent.com/blog/[slug]`
